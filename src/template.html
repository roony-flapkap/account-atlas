Account Atlas

One accumulating map of the portal. Search a company and it is walked, drawn and kept; search one already on the map and the view travels to it. A record on two accounts is one node joining two hives — which is the thing a table of duplicates cannot show you.

Map · checking… HubSpot · unverified

What it does. Walks one company's association graph — its contacts, deals and leads, how those link to each other, and what any of them reach outside the account — then merges the result into a map that is kept between sessions. Walking a second company that shares a contact draws the two as one figure joined by that person.

Reads only. Everything comes through your own HubSpot connector with your permissions; nothing is written back to HubSpot and no credentials touch this page. The map itself is stored against this artifact and is readable by everyone it is shared with.

Caps on one walk: 200 contacts, 200 deals, 200 leads — HubSpot's own ceiling on a page of results — plus 40 objects in the link mesh, 20 lead-to-deal reads, 16 records reached outside the account and 5 phone probes. A walk that hits one says so in its console and on the account's entry, so a partial picture is never a silent one. Expand search · all opens out every record shown that has not been opened yet — three reads each, 40 results a read, at most 60 records a press — and goes one level further out each time it is pressed. Times are UTC, as HubSpot's API returns them — not the UTC+4 that CSV exports render.
